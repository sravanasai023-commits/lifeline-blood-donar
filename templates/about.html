About LifeLink

LifeLink is a Blood Donor Finder platform that connects blood donors with patients and hospitals during emergencies.

Why Donate Blood?

Every blood donation can save up to three lives. Blood cannot be manufactured, so voluntary donors are essential.

Eligibility

  • Age: 18–60 years
  • Weight: Minimum 50 kg
  • Good general health
  • No recent major illness

Benefits of Blood Donation

  • Helps save lives
  • Supports emergency healthcare
  • Encourages community service
  • Promotes regular health checkups

Our Mission

To create a reliable and easy-to-use platform that helps patients quickly find blood donors during emergencies.
